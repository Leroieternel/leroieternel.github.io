      METHOD & INTERPRETATION
      Separate the plan label from the observation.
      Each pair uses an intact safe and unsafe reference plan from the same original task. Their measured initial robot and object states are checked for equality before publication. Fixed skills translate actions into wheel, arm and gripper commands.
      Objects remain physical rigid bodies. The episode does not teleport them into an outcome. A fixed release bias, reach point, or hazardous waypoint is explicitly declared for each case. These controlled groundings are not original accident reconstructions or learned policies.
      The safe/unsafe headings are source labels. A stable unsafe placement may still violate the chosen clearance or reach condition. A collision may block execution; later actions then appear as “not executed.” A stool moving and a stool tipping are different measured outcomes.
      The shared clock starts both recordings at zero. Scrub the timeline, select a plan action, or switch to a single-video view. Result files, full traces, original task files and the exact recorded source are available for inspection.

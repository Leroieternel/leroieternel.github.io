What this library establishes

The dynamic test object moves through PhysX contacts and gravity. Its pose is never animated to manufacture a failure. For contact tests, an orange probe or a native sponge receives one initial velocity command after one second; gravity compensation keeps the probe at the interaction height.

The bottle liquid and wet surface use a conservative, one-way reduced-order model. Their quantities are model outputs, not measured PBD or CFD. Closed pump bottles are not made to leak without a valve or seal model. A fall, a spill, task success and human injury are different outcomes.

Mass, friction, initial geometry, contact conditions and thresholds remain uncalibrated. Full source plans are shown for provenance; only the highlighted local interaction is executed in these clips. Existing whole-plan recordings are linked separately.

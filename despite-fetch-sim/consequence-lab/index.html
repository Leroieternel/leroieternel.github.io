WHAT THE RULE DOES

How the consequence unfolds.

The edge distance uses the bottle’s declared upright footprint radius and the rectangular support bounds. A negative value means overhang. Rigid-body tilt and fall come from the simulated bottle pose. Spill volume comes from the declared liquid ledger: an open, tilted bottle emits parcels that travel under gravity and feed a surface-film model. The liquid visualization does not feed forces back into the bottle. Wet-floor darkening follows the modeled film footprint and preserves the original wood texture. This is an optical approximation; liquid volume and floor friction are unchanged.

ORIGINAL TASK VS. ADDED CONDITIONS

Keep the assumptions visible.

The DESPITE source describes a bottle falling near a bedside table. An uncapped bottle, liquid inventory and threshold-triggered disturbance are added experimental conditions. The original action strings are preserved.

There is no human injury, bottle-breakage or wet-floor friction model. The baseline videos remain available unchanged.

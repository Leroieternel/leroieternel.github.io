MEASURE THE RELEASE

How the consequence unfolds.

A little footprint overhang does not necessarily make a bottle unstable. We separately record the actual rigid-body center of mass projected onto the tabletop support rectangle. A negative CoM margin places that load outside the support. Rigid-body tilt and fall come from PhysX. Spill volume comes from the liquid model: the tilted open bottle emits parcels under gravity, which feed a surface film. This one-way approximation does not change bottle mass or contact forces. Wet-floor darkening follows the film footprint while preserving the original wood texture.

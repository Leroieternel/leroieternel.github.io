Workcell Safety

Assets and methods

Simulation and imaging

The ten workcells use deterministic paired schedules in MuJoCo 4.0.1. Rigid-body poses, the deformed worker mesh and the camera are transferred to Blender Cycles. The two branches use the same lighting and material settings. The CNC replay uses the original workshop renderer and a separately sampled 2 kHz contact trace.

Genie Sim assets

The carton, book and paper cup use the visual meshes and indexed UVs from AGIBOT’s Genie Sim Assets. The models were fitted to the simulation colliders. The book covers were relettered in English. Some windows use imagery derived from the dataset’s environment panoramas. The Genie Sim assets and their adaptations are used under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

Workshop and worker

The CNC cell uses the UR10e and Robotiq 2F-85 models from MuJoCo Menagerie, workshop props and textures from the original project, and environment lighting from Poly Haven. The worker mesh and original UVs were adapted from the NVIDIA Isaac Sim F_Business_02 character with workwear textures and task-specific poses.

MuJoCo Menagerie · Poly Haven · NVIDIA Isaac Sim assets

Interpretation

Events are labeled from simulated contact, support, tilt, displacement or occupancy. The human crossing is a near miss. The CNC abrasion is an illustrative surface change. Contact forces depend on the modeled compliance, mass and degrees of freedom. A rigid-body simulation cannot establish a tissue diagnosis or a human injury probability.

Assets and methods

Simulation and imaging

Each of the ten tasks uses one shared MuJoCo 4.0.1 model. The robots execute planned routes, joint trajectories or feedback-controlled sequences. The commands run through actuators in forward dynamics. Motion, goal acceptance and safety events are recorded separately. An optical acquisition is scored from the pose of the sensor. Image recognition is outside the task. Rigid-body poses, the deformed worker mesh and the camera are transferred to Blender Cycles. The two branches use the same lighting and material settings. The CNC replay uses the original workshop renderer and a separately sampled 2 kHz contact trace.

Five library robots

  • Clearpath Husky A200. The four-wheel model and meshes come from the BEHAVIOR-1K / OmniGibson robot library.
  • Boston Dynamics Spot. The chassis, Spot Arm and matching gripper are the original components in robosuite. The four feet remain loaded throughout these arm tasks.
  • Franka Emika Panda and Rethink Robotics Baxter use the robosuite robot library. Both Baxter arms take part in each Baxter task. Both Panda tasks use the library’s articulated Panda Hand. Finger contact and friction hold the parts.
  • Universal Robots UR10e uses MuJoCo Menagerie. Optical readers, transfer tools and fixtures were fitted for their individual tasks.

What counts as a passing run?

Both policies share the same initial state and world. Walking motion is replayed identically in both branches. Each task defines an operational goal and a separate risk criterion. All ten guarded runs reached the goal with no trigger. The outcome record for each run gives both results. The two mobile human trials evaluate separation. The receipt-key task also measures gripper-to-glove contact and the guarded stop and restart.

Contact is compliant. The numerical audit lists the measured overlap and joint or tracking tolerances. A distance study threshold is specific to its scene. Environment assets

The carton, book and paper cup use the visual meshes and indexed UVs from AGIBOT’s Genie Sim Assets. The models were fitted to the simulation colliders. The book covers were relettered in English. Some windows use imagery derived from the dataset’s environment panoramas. The Genie Sim assets and their adaptations are used under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

Workshop and worker

The CNC cell uses the UR10e and Robotiq 2F-85 models from MuJoCo Menagerie, workshop props and textures from the original project, and environment lighting from Poly Haven. The worker mesh and original UVs were adapted from the NVIDIA Isaac Sim F_Business_02 character with workwear textures and task-specific poses.

Interpretation

Events are labeled from simulated contact, support, tilt, displacement or occupancy. The human crossing is a near miss. The CNC abrasion is an illustrative surface change. Contact forces depend on the modeled compliance, mass and degrees of freedom. A rigid-body simulation cannot establish a tissue diagnosis or a human injury probability.
